Good questions

Everything you need to know before you add it.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, no account, no trial.

Does it collect my data?

No. It runs entirely in your browser. Settings sync through your own Chrome profile.

How is the spacing score worked out?

It reads every margin, padding and gap, detects a 4px or 8px grid, and scores how many values sit on it.

Does it work on localhost?

Yes, on any http or https page, including your dev server.

Which browsers?

Chrome, plus Chromium browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store, like Edge, Brave and Arc.

I used 1.0. What changes?

Your colors and settings carry over. Blue is still margin, green is still padding.
